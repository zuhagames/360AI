# 360AI 2.7 - 100 more offline replies

Exactly **100 new predefined answers** bring the starter pack to **250**.

- **Five new topics:** gaming ideas, tech basics, creative prompts, planning and conversation, with 20 original replies each.
- **Find and ask:** open Home > Y > Saved answers, click the left stick for Starter answers, then START to search. Click the right stick to ask the selected question; B returns to your results.
- **Controller browsing:** D-pad Left/Right now follows actual category boundaries, so the original groups of 15 and new groups of 20 all work together.
- **Instant local replies:** additions share the existing exact-phrase index without loading model weights. Your 16 personal-answer slots remain separate; existing custom replies keep their normal priority.

The original 150 starters, 106 phrase variants and four bundled model weights remain included. This update expands the instant assistant; GGUF generation speed is unchanged.

**Already on 2.1-2.6?** Open **START on Home > Updates** for the small app-only download. For a first installation or 2.0/older, extract **360AI-2.7-USB.zip** and copy its entire `360AI` folder to your drive. App-only updates preserve models, notes, personal answers, settings and custom rules.

Validation: Xbox SDK app/installer builds; native assistant/Hub/engine/updater/controller regressions; all 250 exact replies and search ranks, case/punctuation handling, no-action behavior, new-category searches and variable-size category boundaries; tokenizer/protocol/package checks; native UI captures; package/model hashes and a previous-version install/recovery trial. Physical Xbox menus, keyboard, persistence, networking and inference remain hardware validation tasks.
