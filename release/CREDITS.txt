360AI
https://github.com/zuhagames/360AI

360AI is a fork of Canyons' gemma.xex:
https://gitlab.com/canyons0/gemma.xex

Credit for the original Xbox 360 port belongs to Canyons and the original
project's contributors. 360AI expands the project beyond Gemma with Llama
and Qwen2/2.5 execution paths, GPU matrix calculations and MCP integration.

The original project describes itself as a port of Google's gemma.cpp:
https://github.com/google/gemma.cpp

Implementation references and compatibility documentation are linked in
the 360AI repository README. The USB ZIP includes Google's Gemma 3 270M
Instruct weights in a Q8_0 GGUF published by Second State, redistributed
unchanged. Its model terms, notice, provenance and checksum are included
in the USB ZIP.

SmolLM2 135M Instruct by Hugging Face, with the lmstudio-community Q8_0 GGUF
conversion, is also included unchanged. See SMOLLM2_INFO.txt and APACHE-2.0.txt.
Optional Qwen2.5 model sources/checksums are in models.json (Apache 2.0).
TinyStories 15M Q4_0 and 260K F32 by Andrej Karpathy, converted to GGUF by
ggml-org, are included unchanged. See TINYSTORIES_INFO.txt and
TINYSTORIES_LICENSE.txt (MIT). These are story toys, not chat assistants.
AI tools assisted with development, debugging and documentation.
