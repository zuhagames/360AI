These are deliberately very weak story-completion toys, not instruction-tuned
assistants. Write a short English story opening, such as Once upon a time.
260K may produce mostly nonsense. They cannot replace SmolLM2 for conversation.
360AI uses plain text continuation for their metadata/vocabulary fingerprints,
without Llama Chat markers. Context still needs RAM. No Xbox speed is promised.
