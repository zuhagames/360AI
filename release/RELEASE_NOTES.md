# 360AI 2.8 - Find your next answer

Browse the instant assistant's 250 replies with fewer keyboard trips.

- **Answer topics:** press BACK in Starter answers to choose one of 15 topics, All answers, or Recently asked. A opens your choice; B returns without changing your list.
- **Recently asked:** revisit up to 12 different starter questions asked with right-stick click, newest first. Reasking moves a question to the front when you reopen the list. X on Recently asked in the picker clears recents for this app session.
- **Keep your place:** topic/recent lists page by six with D-pad Left/Right. B from an asked question returns to the same displayed list and selection. Y clears a filter, while cancelled or invalid searches keep it.
- **Less repeated search work:** normalized question/reply/category text is cached after the first nonempty search. Whole-word matching, exact-first ranking and query limits are preserved; lower-priority fields are skipped after a word matches.

Recents use no personal-answer slots or disk writes. All 250 starters, the existing 106 phrase variants, personal replies and four bundled models remain included. This update improves native browsing/search; GGUF generation speed is unchanged.

**Already on 2.1-2.7?** Open **START on Home > Updates** for the small app-only download. For a first installation or 2.0/older, extract **360AI-2.8-USB.zip** and copy its entire `360AI` folder to your drive. App-only updates preserve models, notes, personal answers, settings and custom rules.
