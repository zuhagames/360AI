Xbox 360 controller prompts
==========================
Artwork: Nicolae (Xelu) Berbece. Pack updated by Paul Paun (2021).
Source: https://thoseawesomeguys.com/prompts/
License: CC0 / public domain, as stated on the source page and pack readme.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

360AI embeds resized versions of the original Xbox 360 A, B, X, Y, LB, RB,
Start, Back, D-pad and stick prompts in its native UI texture atlas. The
original PNGs and pack readme are preserved in the source assets/input folder.
No network connection or separate image files are needed on the console.

The artwork is third-party controller illustration. Xbox and Xbox 360 are
Microsoft trademarks; this project is not endorsed by Microsoft.
